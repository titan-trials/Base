Addendum 2: lab_pitcher_form.py re-run (the last one outstanding)

11,148 starts, up from 4,599, now reading cache/distilled/ like the rest.

  - The velocity marker HOLDS and strengthens: r = +0.0815 at z = +8.59
    against a 200-permutation null, surviving the seasonal-arc control and
    still not predicting batters faced. Worth +0.36 K over 23 batters at
    2 sd off a pitcher's own norm -- comparable to the K_PRIOR_BF tilt, and
    now a candidate to wire into the model rather than only display.
  - Recent K rate reverses from noise (r = +0.019, p = 0.21) to real
    (r = +0.0335, z = +3.54) -- but does NOT survive alongside velocity.
    The two correlate at +0.19; fitted jointly, recent K rate falls to
    z = +1.91 and +0.08 K while velocity is untouched. Building the marker
    on velocity and not on recent strikeout rate was the right call, and is
    still the right call for a better reason.
  - The single-shuffle control is replaced with a 200-permutation null. One
    draw at this sample size has an SE near 0.0095 and it drew +0.0183,
    which reads like contamination and was not. A one-draw control is not
    conservative, just noisy.
