THE REAL MISS (CONTEXT V12.9b)

Total error +0.53 K decomposes as workload +1.40 batters and rate ~0. The
workload number is NOT a level error: starts of 15+ BF (90.1%) overshoot
by +0.10, starts under 15 (9.9%) by +13.23. Lowering the level would make
90% of starts worse.

The PIT on k_dist gives chi-square 54.1 on 9 df -- bottom decile 16.7%
against 10% expected. A left-tail problem, at every threshold:

    K <= 1   said 5.21%   happened  9.96%   +4.75% +/- 0.98
    K <= 2   said 14.10%  happened 21.51%   +7.41% +/- 1.51
    K <= 4   said 43.40%  happened 51.20%   +7.79% +/- 2.10

Every under is under-priced by 4-8 points. Bigger than either shipped
change. bf_pmf's own docstring names the mechanism: the non-opener
league_pmf support "starts around thirteen batters" and tilting "cannot
pull a distribution below its own support". 7.6% of real starts finish
under 13. Design not settled; nothing changed.
