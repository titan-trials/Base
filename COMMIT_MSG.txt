Warn on low-line strikeout edges; five hypotheses tested and rejected

The strikeout probabilities are over-confident, worst where the Pitchers
tab prints its biggest edges: on 2.5 and 3.5 lines the model has said
73.8% and it has happened 60.3%, and every probability band reads
negative. Five candidate explanations were tested against the caches and
all five failed, so the symptom is now reported with no cause attached.

What was tested and rejected
  - Shrinkage too strong. lab_shrinkage.py sweeps K_PRIOR_BF over 4,198
    starts; 250 is the measured minimum, and soft arms BEAT their own
    rate (4.29 actual vs 3.83 unshrunk).
  - Distribution too narrow. Standardised residual variance 0.957 +/-
    0.080.
  - Projection above recent form predicts a miss. r = -0.045, p = 0.657.
  - Times through the order. Real in baseball: -3.46 points of K rate on
    the third look, z = -8.84 over 23k same-hitter/same-game matchups.
    Already absorbed by the model, because k_rate is total K over total
    BF across all three looks, so a correct look factor must preserve the
    mean and then moves a printed probability by 0.2 points. No
    per-pitcher version either: split-half r = -0.025, and arsenal depth
    predicts nothing.
  - Bad-and-short coupling. r = -0.041, the wrong SIGN -- long outings
    carry LOWER rates, not higher.

The opposing lineup, closed
lab_log5.py tests whether log5 combines a hitter and a pitcher correctly
-- never previously checked. It does: +0.03% at z = -0.55 over 287,984
plate appearances, bounding any effect under 0.05 K a start. With
lab_opponent.py and lab_tto.py that closes all four opponent questions.
The model's treatment of the opposing lineup is sound, and that is now
measured rather than assumed.

Sample-size discipline
Every lab above first ran against 50 statcast caches because that is what
sat beside it; this machine has 196. No error, no warning. lab_log5.py is
the demonstration -- -0.72% at z = -1.27 on 50 caches, +0.03% at z =
-0.55 on all 196, and at the larger sample the smaller result would have
shown at z = -3.2 had it been real.

  - distill_caches.py reduces 1.25 GB to the ~4 MB the labs consume
    (starts, matchups, arsenal) and distilled.py loads it, PRINTING the
    row count every time. A silent loader is how this stayed invisible.
    Each lab still applies its own MIN_BF after loading; skipping that
    moved lab_shrinkage from 4,198 starts to 4,408.
  - Every affected lab now carries a banner marking its published figures

  - pull_caches.py adds caches ranked by how often a starter has appeared

Addendum after re-running on all 196 caches

distill_caches.py cut them to 4.3 MB and every lab was re-run on the full
set. Three conclusions from the body of this message reverse.

  - K_PRIOR_BF = 250 IS TOO STRONG. On the model's own trailing-12-month
    window shape, 9,723 starts, negative meaning over-projection:

        prior      soft       mid     power
          150   -0.049K   -0.081K   -0.019K
          250   -0.204K   -0.083K   +0.146K   <- live

    A 0.35 K spread between arm types at 250; 0.03 K at 150. Soft arms are
    exactly the pitchers carrying 2.5 and 3.5 lines, and 0.204 K there is
    worth ~3 points of probability at a 3.5 line -- the first located
    component of the -13.5 point low-line gap. Not changed here; a live
    model constant gets a design pass first.
  - "Soft arms BEAT their own rate" was a 50-cache artifact. At 196 they
    come in at 3.97 K against a shrunk projection of 4.11. The answer
    given on Brandon Young is withdrawn.
  - The per-pitcher order penalty DOES persist: split-half r = -0.025
    (z = -0.16) on 42 pitchers became +0.2105 (z = +2.05) on 115, spread
    at z = +3.53. Arsenal depth predicts it -- mix entropy r = +0.209,
    pitch types r = +0.190, fastball share r = -0.128, all consistent. And
    the penalty is multiplicative, not additive (z = -2.84; keep 0.842 of
    the rate, fitting 8x better than a flat subtraction).

The "already absorbed" conclusion does NOT reverse -- it is structural.
k_rate spans all three looks, so any look factor must preserve the mean
and then only redistributes. Still 0.2 points of probability.

lab_coupling.py stayed dead: r = -0.0662 at z = -6.17, wrong sign.

lab_pitcher_form.py has not been re-run and is still reading raw caches.
