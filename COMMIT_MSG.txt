Hitter row log, so the hitter props can be audited the way the pitchers are

cache/scoring_log.csv keeps one row per PROP per slate -- n, base_rate,
mean_pred, auc, brier. That says whether a prop works and nothing else. It
cannot answer "is the model calibrated at the TOP of its range", because by
the time a slate is summarised every individual probability has been
averaged away.

That is exactly the question that mattered on the pitcher side. The
strikeout props read fine in aggregate and were 13.5 points hot in the
65-80% band, and the only reason anyone could see it is that
pitcher_row_log.csv stores the per-start distribution. The hitter side had
no equivalent, so the same failure would have been invisible: a prop can
read said 11.5% / happened 11.5% across 3,538 rows while the handful of
rows above 30% -- the ones a LOTTO leg is actually picked from -- run
badly hot.

score_slate._log_hitter_rows writes one row per clean hitter-game to
cache/hitter_row_log.csv: identity, lineup slot, every prob_* the slate
carried, every outcome it can be graded against, and the hot/cold marker so
it can grade itself here the way the pitcher markers do. Clean rows only,
same contract as the pitcher log. About 220 rows a slate, ~25 KB a night.

Re-scoring a slate REPLACES its rows rather than appending them, matching
scoring_log and pitcher_row_log. score_slate is safe to re-run by design --
odds arrive late, boxscores get corrected -- and a log that grew on every
re-run would silently weight those nights twice, with every calibration
number drawn from it wrong in a way nothing would flag. test_hitter_log.py
(14 checks) covers that case specifically, plus empty/None frames and a
slate missing columns.

Prompted by asking whether HR belongs anywhere but a LOTTO slip. It does
not -- tier_of is pure probability and HR clears the 28% MEDIUM cut on 6 of
1,367 slate rows -- but the premise that HR is a coin flip was false and
came from a V2 TRAINING result (AUC 0.51-0.54) that the per-PA rate engine
superseded. Graded live, prob_hr has the HIGHEST AUC of any hitter prop:
0.625 mean per slate over 16 slates, sd 0.067, z = +7.5, above 0.500 on 15
of 16, calibrated at said 11.52% / happened 11.50%. CONTEXT.md now carries
a correction above the stale bullet.

by date, so it is safe.
